FitBond - Account & Data Deletion Request

At FitBond, we respect your privacy and grant you full control over your personal information. You can request the permanent deletion of your FitBond account and all associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Delete Account via Mobile App (Recommended)

  1. Open the FitBond app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on your Profile icon at the bottom right.
  3. Go to Edit Profile / Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the bottom and select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm deletion. Your account credentials and data will be removed immediately.

Option 2: Submit a Manual Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to log in, you can request manual account deletion from our support team:

Send an email to: support@fitbond.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]

Details to include: Your full name, registered email address, and username used on FitBond.

Data Deletion & Retention Details

  • Data Permanently Removed: Your account profile, email address, password hash, profile avatar, chat history, shared snaps, and fitness streak records.
  • Processing Timeline: In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests are processed within 7 to 30 days.
  • Data Retention: Non-personally identifiable analytical logs or required legal/compliance records may be retained as permissible by applicable laws.
