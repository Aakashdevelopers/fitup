Privacy Policy for FitBond

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

Welcome to FitBond ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use our FitBond mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Account Information

When you register or sign up for FitBond, we may collect:

  • Account Credentials: Full Name, Email Address, Password (encrypted), and Profile Bio/Picture.
  • OAuth Data: Google Sign-In details (Name, Email, Profile Avatar) when you authenticate via Google Sign-In.

B. Health & Fitness Data (Fitness Info)

  • Fitness Info: Workout logs, total workouts completed, workout streak counts, fitness goals, and workout activity tracking to power your AI Workout Coach and streak milestones.

C. Photos, Videos, and Audio Files

  • Photos & Videos: Camera access for fitness snap capture, profile pictures, and gallery images shared with friends.
  • Voice or Sound Recordings: Audio captured via the microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) for real-time audio/video calls (TRTC) and voice messaging features.

D. Device and Usage Data

  • Identifiers & Messaging: Network connectivity state, device model, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: AI workout coach, streak tracking, social fitness snaps, private chat, and real-time calling.
  • To authenticate your identity securely via Email/Password or OAuth.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding workout streaks, friend requests, and messages.
  • To process account maintenance and user support requests.

3. Account & Data Deletion Policy

Your Right to Erase Data: You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your FitBond account and all associated personal data at any time.

Request Account Deletion

How to delete your account:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile > Edit Profile / Settings > Delete Account inside the FitBond mobile app.
  • Web / Email Request: If you cannot access the app, you can submit an account deletion request via our Account Deletion Request Page or email us directly at support@fitbond.com.

What Data Gets Erased: Your account profile, registered email address, authentication credentials, friend list, chat messages, and shared workout snaps will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of request.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase: Secure authentication, cloud storage, and push notifications.
  • Google OAuth: Secure third-party single sign-on services.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS, Firebase Security Rules) to safeguard your information. However, no digital transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
